I'm not an expert in philosophy, just a hobbyist. But I hardly believe it's still useful to study. Maybe it's fun to read, like a story. But I just cannot believe it's a profession that still gets serious treatments.

My argument is always the same: science has been taking over philosophy's lunch and there is nothing philosophy can do about it. Philosophers can pretend that they are pursuing more and more abstract or irrelevant subjects, but in the end, somehow, someday, it's observed, formalized, analyzed, and explained. Well, what about those things that are beyond observation? We can and it's in the human nature that we speculate, but it's hardly mean anything but an interesting thought beyond speculation.

I do feel that I'm a bit arrogant when writing these words down. I do feel sorry, but I don't find any evidence to say otherwise.
