I sometimes feel that programmers and software engineers are two different kinds of people. Programmers are essentially puzzle lovers and software engineers are problem solvers. Granted many programmers become software engineers in profession and many software engineers become programmers for fun. In many ways, I identify myself as a programmer first. Maybe I'm not a very good one, but it's really fun to figure out puzzles that have definitive answers and learn something from it. And I feel that I have similar passion over most subjects I love. I don't care that much that I cannot innovate. Having fun is always the most important aspect of doing something, even if I have to struggle sometimes.
