I think I will be asked by my relatives about whether studying programming is still necessary and surely they will also have many opinions in the coming days, so I will just write it here a bit:

So, to me, learning how to program or studying algorithms, data structures, and statistics is not about employability or career choosing or productivity maxxing. I was just curious about things. I was not a very good student who would learn well under massive amount of exercises (that kills my curiosity in most cases), but I was genuinely curious about science, machines, and crafts. And that experience shapes my philosophy and view of everything. So, yes, if a kid is as curious as I am, he/she should absolutely learn these things, these are some real fascinating machinery.
