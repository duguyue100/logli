The first version of Warcraft III I played is v1.19. And I still remember the first map I played isn't the official release. And it even isn't the popular customized map. Soonly my friends and I turned to what we called "regular match". We formed our team, we practiced together, we held unofficial contests. I picked Undead out of four races. I learned how to control the match, I learned how to control my temper, I learned how to attack at the right time. And I remember the version of that era is v1.20. And then we all went to high school. That's the time we started to lose our interests on this game. We read more, we played less, we call off the team, we stopped practices. That is the age of v1.22, a good news to all UDer. Then I turned and trained myself to a programmer. Thanks to the old days experience, so I can code fast nowadays. I remember that WCG was the biggest thing in our life every year. We were so happy when Xiaofeng Li won the first Champion for China on WCG 2005. Today is the end of WCG 2013, and also the end of Warcraft III to be the official game on WCG. TH000 keeps the Champion for China. Moon, the greatest elf player, "the fifth race", won the second place. Well, I shouldn't be sad because Warcraft III is still there for other contests. But today is still the end of an era. The one my friends and I would value in rest of our life.
