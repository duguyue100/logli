I bought a pair of Keychron Z11 Ultra, which is my first split keyboard. I think it's a good design. I would have more keys than 68. But hey, what can you do right?     I have to do some key mapping and recycle some my old key caps, but overall works well.

The most troubling thing is actually that I couldn't have a pure wireless design. The package collisions between left and right pads is almost too much for me to handle. This can be solved if I connect the left and right pads with a USB-C cable, but to be honest, it's not a very pretty layout. I hope they will do it better in updating drivers.
